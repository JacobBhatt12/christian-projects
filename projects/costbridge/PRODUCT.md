# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People trying to make essential expenses more manageable, often on a phone and with limited time or attention. Community organizations and trusted reviewers also use the product to suggest, correct, approve, and verify assistance information.

## Product Purpose

CostBridge helps people compare lower-cost options and find nearby community support for groceries, housing, utilities, transportation, healthcare, childcare, clothing, internet, and phone service. Success means a person can enter a ZIP code and need, understand honest tradeoffs, save useful results, and take a clear next step without needing an account or surrendering precise location data.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, trustworthy, and steady. The voice uses plain language, respects the user's agency, and treats seeking a better price or community help as an ordinary, practical act.

## Anti-references

CostBridge must not resemble a predatory lead-generation site, a financial product sales funnel, a bureaucratic benefits portal, or a glossy startup dashboard. Avoid urgency tactics, qualification promises, shame-based language, decorative gradients, glass effects, crowded card grids, and institutional jargon.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next useful action within easy reach.
2. Show the source and freshness of information so trust is earned.
3. Explain tradeoffs and eligibility carefully, without implying certainty.
4. Reduce cognitive load with progressive disclosure and familiar controls.
5. Preserve privacy by default and collect only what the task needs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations with visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive labels, screen-reader announcements, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, large touch targets, and readable text. Never rely on color alone. Clearly label sample or unverified data and support multiple languages in resource information.

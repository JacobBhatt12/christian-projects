A Guide For Sharing Your Faith

The Romans Road

Five verses from the book of Romans, in order, with a simple line to say at each one. Tap through the guide, then use the closing prayer when someone is ready to respond.

What to say

Invite them to pray this in their own words — it's the posture of the prayer that matters, not the exact wording.

"God, I know I've fallen short and I can't fix that on my own.

Thank You for sending Jesus to die for me and rise again.

I believe that, and I want to trust Him with my life.

Jesus, be my Lord and my Savior. Thank You for Your gift of life. Amen."

Verses shown in the ESV. Keep a Bible or Bible app on hand so they can read each verse themselves.

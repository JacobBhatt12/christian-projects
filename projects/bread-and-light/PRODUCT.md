# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Christians who have a small, honest amount of time and want one concrete, safe way to care for someone. They may be using the app quietly at home or between ordinary responsibilities. The primary task is to choose available time, receive a realistic service idea, act, and later record a private reflection.

## Product Purpose

Bread & Light turns available time into one manageable act of service, then gets out of the way. Success means a person can choose a time, receive and change an idea without repetition, commit, return later, and keep a private reflection stored only on the device.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, and sincere. The voice is conversational and encouraging without guilt, ambition, spiritual competition, or productivity language. The experience should feel like a thoughtful community handout or a well-used personal journal translated into a restrained desktop tool.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a startup analytics dashboard, gamified habit tracker, social network, glossy church campaign, stock-image devotional app, or a design built from decorative religious cliches. Avoid accounts, profiles, sharing, scores, points, streaks, badges, leaderboards, progress metrics, clocks, timers, glossy gradients, glass effects, rounded cards, and ornamental religious imagery.

## Design Principles

1. Offer one useful next step, then recede.
2. Honor small acts without measuring or performing them.
3. Keep private words visibly and technically private.
4. Make safety and personal boundaries part of faithful action.
5. Prefer specific, ordinary help over vague inspiration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, correct labels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, high contrast, practical 44-pixel targets, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from 320 pixels upward. Never rely on color alone. Respect the user's relationships, abilities, circumstances, boundaries, and another person's answer.

# Belong Here

## Register

product

## Users

People who are exploring, visiting, or newly attending a church, including individuals, couples, families, students, people with disabilities, and people who may be unsure about faith. Approved church leaders and trained volunteers use a separate administrative surface.

## Product Purpose

Belong Here helps someone move from “I visited a church” to “I know where I belong, what my next step is, and who I can connect with.” Success means a newcomer can reduce first-visit uncertainty, choose a comfortable next step, and connect safely while remaining in control of their privacy.

## Brand Personality

Warm, peaceful, and human. Encouraging, plainspoken, inclusive, and free of pressure.

## Anti-references

- Dating apps, open social networks, and follower mechanics
- Corporate church-management software on the member side
- Formal religious ornament and insider language
- Manipulative urgency, popularity scores, and gamified faith
- Generic pastel SaaS layouts and interchangeable card grids

## Design Principles

1. Reduce uncertainty before asking for commitment.
2. Offer clear next steps, never pressure.
3. Make human connections church-mediated and consent-based.
4. Keep personal information private until the user chooses to share it.
5. Prefer practical, specific information over promotion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with high contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, multilingual readiness, and filters for childcare, wheelchair access, sensory needs, and interpretation.
